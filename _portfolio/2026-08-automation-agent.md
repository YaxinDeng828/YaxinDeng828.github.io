Helped build GraphLoop, a GraphEngineering-based experiment decision system that organizes methods, results, applicability conditions, and real costs into an extensible experiment-path graph. It generates deepening, ablation, combination, and exploration paths, then prioritizes them using historical returns, Information Gain, expected time, and instability risk.

Reflection, Evidence, Recovery, and Memory Agents support hypothesis clarification, conflicting-evidence analysis, anomaly recovery, and experience write-back. With only nine online feedback opportunities, the system improved classification, recommendation, and total scores by 8.33%, 54.66%, and 21.01%.
