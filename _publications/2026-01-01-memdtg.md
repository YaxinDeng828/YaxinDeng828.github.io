### Abstract

MemDTG represents long-term agent memory as a temporal network with time, provenance, and evolution relationships. It introduces temporal evidence admission and auditable retrieval mechanisms that filter stale, future, or question-irrelevant evidence before ranking, while tracing the source of each answer.

Against representative long-term memory systems including Mem0, MemGPT, and Zep on LongMemEval and LoCoMo, MemDTG achieved LLM evaluation accuracy of 79.96% / 69.78% and Top-5 evidence recall of 78.68% / 54.01%.
